🔴 Exam Mode
Blocks AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude etc.) using DNS filtering. Students cannot access blocked sites even if they change their DNS settings.
⛔ Kill All Internet
Instantly cuts ALL internet access for every student on the network. Use in emergencies or at the start of an exam. Click Restore to bring everyone back online.
🔒 Strict Mode
Allows ONLY Google Classroom and Google Docs. All other websites including social media and games are blocked. Use during open-book exams where only Classroom access is allowed.
🚫 Block Device
Cuts internet for one specific student device only. Other students are not affected. Click Unblock to restore that device.
🔄 Refresh Devices
Scans the network for newly connected devices. Use if a student's device is not showing in the list.
